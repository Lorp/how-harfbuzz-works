# Serial commas added, 20 November 2026

The book switched to the serial comma (“A, B, and C”) on 20 November 2026, following Behdad Esfahbod’s note on the foreword. Quotations, code, and the titles of other works keep their own punctuation. Each line below is a line number and the text around the new comma, made from `git diff` against commit 9f8b186 (after PR #3).

## Other changes made at the same time

* `bibliography.md:52`: “#1924 and #2061, and #539, which…” became “#1924, #2061, and #539, which…” (the line’s two commas aren’t listed below).
* `command-line-tools.md:77`: a stray period removed, “for flags.:” → “for flags:”.
* `command-line-tools.md:384`: the next chapter “writes programs … in C, JavaScript and Rust” → “shows how to write programs … in C, JavaScript, Python, and Rust”: it left out Python.
* `fallbacks.md:306`, `where-metrics-come-from.md:371`: “centring” → “centering”.
* `testing-harfbuzz.md:274`: the hidden comment on bug numbers being permanent.

## Left alone, as not simple lists

* `foreword.md:23`: “my notes, an old essay and some tweets” lacks the comma, but the foreword is Behdad’s text.
* `family.md:23`: “Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign, QuarkXPress and Scribus, as well as…”: commas inside the Adobe group only; the sentence nests lists and may want rewording.
* `joining-scripts.md:145` (“Arabic, Urdu and Sindhi”), `for-applications.md:234–235` (HarfBuzz manual chapter titles): titles of other works.
* Lists nested in lists, or ending in a pair, such as `from-features-to-lookups.md:54` and `:60`, `compile-time-configuration.md:13`, `:91` and `:179`, `fallbacks.md:141`, `subsetting.md:454`, `color.md:270`, `writing-tables.md:325`.
