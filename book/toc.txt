# The order of the book.
#
#   front/title.md        a written page: a path relative to book/
#   ? Title | summary     a planned page, not written yet: the build makes a
#                         short page for it from the title and summary
#   [front]               the sections of the book. Pages in a [part] are
#   [part] Title          chapters, numbered through the whole book; pages
#   [appendices]          in [appendices] are lettered A, B, C…
#   [end]
#
# A page's name (and its fonts folder, fonts/<name>/) is its file name, or
# for a planned page, its title made into a name: “Buffers” is buffers.
